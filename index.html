      Meal Timing

      • Stay consistent with meal times

      Having Trouble Sleeping?

      Establish a Sleep Schedule

      • Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day
      • Avoid sleeping in on weekends
      • Create a consistent bedtime routine
      • Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night

      Optimize Your Sleep Environment

      • Keep your bedroom cool (around 65°F/18°C)
      • Ensure your room is dark and quiet
      • Invest in a comfortable mattress and pillows
      • Use breathable bedding materials

      Things to Avoid

      • Limit screen time 1-2 hours before bed
      • Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
      • Don't eat large meals close to bedtime
      • Reduce alcohol and nicotine intake

      Daytime Habits

      • Exercise regularly (but not too close to bedtime)
      • Get natural sunlight during the day
      • Limit daytime naps to 20-30 minutes
      • Stay active and engaged during the day

      Relaxation Techniques

      • Practice deep breathing exercises
      • Try progressive muscle relaxation
      • Use meditation or mindfulness apps
      • Listen to calming sounds or white noise

      If sleep problems persist, consider consulting a healthcare provider or sleep specialist.
